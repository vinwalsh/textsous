Charred Corn and Lime Salad

Serves 4 as a side dish.

Ingredients

  • 4 ears corn, shucked
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

Instructions

  1. Brush the corn with the olive oil.
  2. Char on a hot grill, turning, until blackened in spots, about 8 minutes.
  3. Cut the kernels from the cobs into a bowl.
  4. Toss with the lime juice, cilantro, and salt. Serve warm.
